This sample demonstrates the mention support capabilities of the AI AssistView control. Mentions can be referenced directly within prompts, enabling intelligent and context-aware interactions.

In this example, the mentions property is used to configure multiple mention sources, enabling users to quickly access contextual actions within the AI AssistView. The bannerTemplate customizes the banner content, and toolbarSettings adds custom toolbar items like a right-aligned Refresh button. The promptRequest event processes the selected mentions and generates responses based on the chosen skills and agents.
